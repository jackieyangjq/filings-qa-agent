# Evaluation results

Three ways of retrieving filing passages, compared on the same 50 questions: BM25 keyword search, dense vector search
and a hybrid of the two. For each question, each strategy retrieves passages, `gemini-3.5-flash-lite` answers from
them with a citation on every sentence, and a second model call grades the answer against a reference answer.

Run on 2026-09-23 over the 48 filings of `companies.yaml` (12 companies, each with its latest 10-K and three latest
10-Qs; 9,814 chunks of about 280 words). The questions are in [`eval/questions.jsonl`](../eval/questions.jsonl); every
answer, grade and ranking is in [`eval/results/2026-09-23.json`](../eval/results/2026-09-23.json).

Recall, section hit, the grades, citation OK and false abstain are over the 40 answerable questions (one question is
2.5 points); abstain OK is over the 10 unanswerable ones. Tokens, latency and cost are per answer call, over all 50
questions; the cost column is the total for the 50 answers at paid-tier prices, while the runs themselves used the
free tier. Definitions are in the appendix.

**What the numbers say**

1. Keyword search (BM25) did best on this set, with 95% of answers correct and the reference passage in the top 5
   for 77.5% of questions; hybrid search came close (87.5% correct, 3 questions behind, which with 40 questions is
   within chance: exact McNemar test, p = 0.25), and dense search alone was clearly worse (67.5% correct, p = 0.001
   against BM25).
2. Dense search misses passages that turn on exact names and figures ("the wafer supply agreement with GF"), after
   which the model declines to answer (25% of answerable questions, against 2.5% with BM25); fused with equal weight,
   those misses also push some keyword hits out of hybrid's top 5 (recall@5 67.5% against 77.5%, while recall@10 is
   85.0% against 87.5%).
3. The set probably flatters BM25, since the questions were written from the passages and reuse their wording, while
   real users paraphrase.
4. The grounding rules held: no sentence cited a passage the model had not been shown, and the model declined 29 of
   the 30 unanswerable question and strategy pairs (the one miss, q46, is a badly worded question about "those
   licenses" in a "Q3 2019 Form 10-Q", which the model answered for NVIDIA's fiscal Q3 2026 instead).
5. Recall understates retrieval, because 4 to 8 answers per strategy were correct without the reference passage: the
   same figure is restated elsewhere (Apple's 2025 net sales of $416,161 million appear in the MD&A, the income
   statement and a note, in rows worded differently).

An answer takes about 4,500 input and 100 output tokens and 1.1 seconds, which at paid-tier prices is $1.50 to $1.70
per 1,000 questions.

## Two failure cases

**1. The passage is never retrieved (q09, all three strategies).** "What percentage of Costco's net sales were
generated by its international operations during 2025?" The answer, 27%, is in one sentence of Costco's 10-K risk
factors ("During 2025, our international operations, including Canada, generated 27% and 34% of our net sales and
operating income."). BM25 ranked Apple's passages about international net sales above it, the other two returned
other Costco and Amazon passages, and none had it in the top 10. Each time the model said the excerpts did not give
the figure: a miss, but an honest one.

**2. A citation that does not support the claim (q06, dense).** "What would have been the additional cost of sales
for Amazon as of December 31, 2025, for every 1% of additional inventory valuation allowance?" The filing's answer is
about $405 million, from the MD&A. Dense search missed that chunk and returned a note stating that the inventory
valuation allowance was $3.3 billion. The model took 1% of that, answered "$33 million" and cited the note. The
citation check passed, because the cited chunk had been shown to the model; it cannot tell whether the chunk supports
the sentence. The judge graded the answer incorrect. BM25 and hybrid search ranked the MD&A chunk first and second,
and both answered $405 million.

## Limitations

- **The questions are model-written.** `gemini-3.5-flash-lite` wrote each question, its reference answer and a quote
  from one chunk; the code checked that the quote is in the chunk, that the question names the company and that it
  is not a yes/no question. Only the questions marked `"reviewed": true` in `eval/questions.jsonl` were checked by
  hand against the filing. Known flaws: q21 gives no period (Costco's letter of credit facilities differ from quarter
  to quarter), and q14 and its rewrite q46 refer to "those licenses" without saying which.
- **The judge is a model too**, from the same family as the model it grades, so it may share its blind spots. A hand
  check of 18 grades (the 6 that were not "correct" and 12 of the 100 "correct" ones, drawn at random) found none
  wrong, but its stated reasons are sometimes off: on q21 it called $242 million against $224 million a transposition
  of digits, when the answer had taken another quarter's figure.
- **The sample is small.** With 40 answerable questions, one question moves a rate by 2.5 points; only the gaps
  between dense search and the other two are clear.
- **One run per strategy**, at the model's default temperature. Answers can differ from run to run; the cache keeps
  this run's answers so that the table can be recomputed.
- **Fact questions only.** Chunks without any number were not used to write questions (the model found nothing to ask
  about in them), so the set is heavy on figures and dates; open questions ("why did margins fall?") are not tested.
- **Section hit is coarse** for JPMorgan and Exxon Mobil, whose filings are not split reliably into sections
  (JPMorgan's 10-Qs have no Item headings), so it is a secondary measure.

## Appendix

### A. How the questions were made

`filings-qa evalset build` (63 model calls) drew chunks by company and section: for ten companies the risk factors
(Item 1A), the MD&A of the 10-K (Item 7) and of the 10-Qs (Item 2) and the financial statements with their notes
(10-K Item 8, 10-Q Item 1), in rotation from one company to the next; for JPMorgan and Exxon Mobil one filing at a
time, the 10-K first. Chunks under 100 words, mostly table rows or without any number were skipped. A chunk whose
question failed a check was replaced by another chunk of the same section: 13 of the 53 chunks tried were rejected,
11 of them because the model found no fact to ask about. The reference passages are every chunk of the company that contains the quote:
chunks overlap, and the 10-Qs repeat text of the 10-K.

| | Questions |
|---|---|
| Answerable | 40: 3 or 4 per company; risk factors 7, 10-K MD&A 9, 10-Q MD&A 9, financial statements 9, JPMorgan and Exxon Mobil by filing 6 |
| Company outside the corpus | 5: an answerable question rewritten for Netflix, Intel, Disney, Pfizer or Boeing |
| Period outside the corpus | 5: an answerable question rewritten for fiscal 2019 |

### B. Metrics

- **Recall@5, Recall@10:** a reference passage is among the first 5 or 10 chunks the strategy ranks. Each strategy
  ranks 10 chunks per question; the answer model is shown the first 8.
- **Section hit@5 (secondary):** a chunk from the same filing and section as a reference passage is in the first 5.
- **Correct, Partial, Incorrect:** the judge's grade of the answer against the reference answer and the quote. An
  abstention counts as incorrect without asking the judge.
- **Citation OK:** of the answer sentences that cite chunks, the share citing a reference passage or a chunk of the
  same filing and section.
- **Abstain OK:** the share of unanswerable questions the model declined. **False abstain:** the share of answerable
  questions it declined.
- **Est. cost:** the 50 answer calls at the paid-tier price of `gemini-3.5-flash-lite`, $0.30 per million input
  tokens and $2.50 per million output tokens including thinking
  ([Gemini API pricing](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/pricing), read 2026-09-23). The judge's 106 calls would
  add $0.02 in all.

### D. Reproducing

```bash
pip install -e '.[llm,embed]'
filings-qa ingest && filings-qa index          # needs SEC_USER_AGENT; see .env.example
filings-qa evalset build                       # writes eval/questions.jsonl; --force to replace it
filings-qa eval --strategies bm25              # then dense, then hybrid; needs GEMINI_API_KEY
filings-qa eval --report-only                  # the table, from the cache in data/cache/eval
```

Each answered and graded question is cached in `data/cache/eval/<strategy>/<qid>.json`, so a run cut short by the
free tier's limits continues where it stopped. On 2026-09-23 the three strategies took 5 to 6 minutes each (256 model
calls: 150 answers and 106 grades); the free tier's per-minute limit was reached 16 times and waited out, and no item
failed.
